Places About Son

Hi, I'm Son

I've lived in Vung Tau for almost a year. This site is my notebook, and you're welcome to use it as a guide for travelling, living and getting through everyday life here.

Why this site exists

I work remotely, and I like living near the sea. Being by the sea makes me feel much more at ease than living in Ho Chi Minh City, so almost a year ago I moved to Vung Tau.

Since then I've eaten at a lot of places and sat in a lot of cafes. Friends and strangers kept asking where to go, so I started writing it down. This is that notebook: restaurants, street food, cafes, hotels and guesthouses, for anyone staying for a weekend or for months, remote workers included.

How I choose

I only mark a place Verified by Son after I've been there myself, and I note the month I visited. Places suggested by readers show as Community until I've checked them. I tell you what to order, what to skip, and whether the wifi is good enough to work.

Nothing hidden

Everything on this site lives in a public file on GitHub, so every change is visible. There is no database and no hidden backend.

Help me add more

Know a place that should be here? Suggest it on GitHub, or edit data/places.json and open a pull request. The guide is in the README.

Area names

Bãi Trước is Front Beach, Bãi Sau is Back Beach, Bãi Dâu is Dau Beach. Phường 1, Phường 2 and Phường Thắng Tam are central districts. I keep the Vietnamese names so you can read them on signs and tell your driver.
