# Surface brief: openvungtau site (home + About)

## Scope and visitor mode
Read. Pages: index.html (places directory, Son's notebook), about.html (Son's welcome). Same shell on both. Five UI languages (en, vi, ko, zh, ru), last choice remembered.

## Audience, job, action, content, constraints
Expats/nomads and tourists in Vũng Tàu, on a phone, picking where to go. They read Son's first-person notes and act: open the map. Content: places.json entries (translated must_try and local_tip). Photos are supplied later by Son: build photo slots that look finished without images, never stock. Static HTML/CSS/JS only. Reading column stays calm; the notebook world owns the frame (page ground, headings, tape, stamps, handwriting accents), not the body text.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A notebook Son keeps for people who stay a while: each place is a page with a ticket, a taped photo and a hand note. Refuses the category default: a bright hero over a grid of identical photo cards.

OWN-WORLD: Ruled notebook paper (#F4E9D6) on a darker desk board (#C9B79A); ink #2B2A33; red-clay accent #C8553D for headings and stamps; sea-glass #6FA6A0 for tape and photo slots. Washi tape, slight rotations (under 4 degrees), white-bordered photo slots, rubber-stamp status marks. Handwriting-style face only for short notes and the greeting, never body. Dark mode: night-blue desk, paper becomes dusky page, same accents lifted.

STORY: The visitor understands in seconds that a real person lived here and recommends only places he visited. They believe it because every entry carries a visit month and Son's own tip. They open a place, read the tip, and tap Open in Google Maps.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop 1440: the page sheet centered 1200 wide on the desk; brand top-left, nav top-right (Places, About, language, theme). Greeting headline in clay-red, rotated about 1 degree, left; two taped photo slots overlapping at right; below the greeting, search and chips; first place card clipped lower-left, with the Son-visited stamp. Phone: sheet fills the width, greeting first, one photo slot, search then chips, then cards; primary action is search and open-card.

FORM: Travel journal with taped photos and stamps, position 1 of my ordered list, the impeccable pick card; seed: seed:e91a79b1f096bbb3.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
